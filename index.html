About Us

Who we are and how the business actually works.

Solar Power was founded on a straightforward premise: the returns produced by renewable generation should not be reserved for institutional balance sheets. Utility-scale solar is among the most predictable asset classes in existence — sunlight is free, panels degrade slowly and measurably, and offtake is contracted years in advance. The economics are dull, and that is precisely the point.

What we operate

We finance, commission and maintain photovoltaic arrays sited across southern and eastern Europe. Each site is selected on irradiance data, grid proximity and permitting clarity. Once a site is energised it feeds the grid under a metered agreement, and the revenue from that meter is what funds distributions. We do not trade, we do not lend, and we do not speculate on price. We sell electricity.

How your allocation works

When you take a capacity plan, your capital is assigned to installed generating capacity. Yield accrues continuously against that allocation rather than being credited in monthly lumps, so your balance reflects production in close to real time. Withdrawals are processed Monday to Friday during business hours — a deliberate constraint that keeps settlement aligned with the banking day rather than promising something we cannot honour at 3am.

On trust

We would rather be measured than admired. Server time governs every accrual, so no device clock can influence a balance. Every allocation, distribution and withdrawal is written to an auditable ledger. Our team is reachable through in-app support and answers in hours, not weeks.

The long view

Panels installed today will still be producing in twenty years. We build the portfolio with that horizon in mind and we would rather grow slowly against real assets than quickly against a promise. If that sounds unexciting, we have described it accurately.

Install the App

Solar Power runs as a native-style app on your phone. No store, no download.

iPhone & iPad
  1. Open this page in Safari. Chrome on iOS cannot install apps.
  2. Tap the Share icon at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Confirm with Add. The icon appears on your home screen.
Android
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm. Solar Power installs like any other app.
Desktop
  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar.
  3. Confirm Install.

Installing adds a shortcut and enables full-screen mode. It requests no permissions and stores nothing beyond your session.
